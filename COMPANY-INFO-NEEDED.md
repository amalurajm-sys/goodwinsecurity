# Company information still required

The website deliberately shows **no** invented company details. Fill these in through **Admin → Company & homepage** (or the section noted) once they are verified. Empty fields are simply hidden on the site.

## Contact (Admin → Company & homepage)
- [ ] Telephone number (shown with a click-to-call link)
- [ ] WhatsApp business number (enables the floating WhatsApp button)
- [ ] Public email address
- [ ] Street address (also published in search-engine structured data)
- [ ] Google Maps embed link (enables the map on the Contact page)
- [ ] Opening hours
- [ ] Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X)
- [ ] Email address that should receive enquiry notifications
- [ ] Whether initial consultations are **free**. If yes, tick the box and the buttons will say "Request a Free Consultation".

## Server (`.env`)
- [ ] SMTP details for the mailbox that sends enquiry notifications (see DEPLOYMENT.md §6)

## Products (Admin → Products)
- [ ] Real product list: manufacturer, model number, SKU, specifications (from datasheets), compatibility notes and photos
- [ ] Remove or unpublish the 16 **sample** products, or edit them and untick "Sample / placeholder"
- [ ] Confirm which brands the company actually supplies (Admin → Brands)
- [ ] Official brand logos, only if the company is permitted to use them

## Projects (Admin → Projects)
- [ ] Real completed projects: title, category, system types, description and photos, with the client's permission to publish
- [ ] General location, only if approved for publication
- [ ] Remove the 3 **sample** projects

## About Us (Admin → Company & homepage → About Us page)
- [ ] Confirm or rewrite the company overview, approach and commitment text
- [ ] Optional, only if accurate: year founded, CR number, team size, certifications, manufacturer partnerships

## Services (Admin → Services)
- [ ] Confirm each of the six services is actually offered; edit or hide any that are not
- [ ] Photos of real installations for each service (optional)

## Branding and imagery
- [ ] Final logo files (the current shield logo can be kept or replaced; see README → Replacing the logo)
- [ ] Homepage banner photo (Admin → Company & homepage → Homepage banner). Without one, an illustration is shown.

## Legal
- [ ] Have the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions reviewed (they are general templates and make no compliance claims)
